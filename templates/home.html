Frequently Asked Questions

Are there any hidden fees or commissions?

None at all. When you sell directly to Apex Bridge Properties, the offer amount we present is the exact amount you walk away with. We cover all standard closing fees, and there are no commissions.

Do I need to clean or make repairs before selling?

No. We buy homes completely as-is, in any physical condition. You can leave unwanted belongings, trash, or needed repairs behind, and we will handle all cleanup and structural work after closing.

Is there any obligation when I submit my address?

There is absolutely zero obligation. Submitting your address simply initiates our research team's valuation. If our cash offer doesn't work for your needs, you can decline it with zero questions asked.
